# Assets

- Webdust PNG: supplied by the project owner; included unchanged.
- 3D geometry, surface textures, offer boards, animation and layout: original procedural project sources.
- `public/assets/market-ambience.ogg`: **Market recorded audio**, Aishatu98, 15 July 2026. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Market_recorded_audio.ogg . Published as CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Bundled unmodified. This is a contemporary field recording used as an optional atmospheric sound bed, not a historically authenticated Arabian recording. Sound is off until the visitor enables it and fades out at the digital reveal.
- React, Vite and Three.js: MIT licenses in their installed packages. GSAP: https://gsap.com/standard-license/ .
